The Core Finding: Association vs. Causation
Financial Metric Positive Mean Negative Mean Welch t-stat p-value Statistically Significant?
Same-Day Return (T₀) +1.028% -0.898% 13.522 3.59 × 10⁻⁴¹ ✅ YES (Massive Co-Movement)
Next-Day Return (T₁) +0.233% +0.244% -0.080 0.936 ❌ NO (Zero Alpha)
5-Day Forward Volatility 4.291% 4.642% -4.736 2.22 × 10⁻⁶ ✅ YES (Panic = Higher Vol)
Intraday Price Range 6.042% 7.029% -8.567 1.30 × 10⁻¹⁷ ✅ YES (Wider Swings)
Key Scientific Takeaway: When stocks rise today, retail investors post optimistic tweets. When stocks plunge, they post panic. However, next-day returns following positive vs negative posts are statistically identical (+0.233% vs +0.244%, p = 0.936). Social media is an emotional mirror, not an oracle.

r = +0.037
p = 0.559 (Spearman ρ = +0.007, p = 0.909)

Predictive OLS regression: R² = 0.00137 (0.14%). Daily sentiment holds zero statistical predictive power for next-day market direction.

Economic Implication: Retail sentiment cannot be used to market-time broad equity indices. By the time retail investors express euphoria or panic, the price move has already been completed.

Real-World Case Studies
1. March 2020 Crash

COVID Liquidity Freeze

  • Negative emotions surged +124% to 443 posts.
  • Bullish share hit annual low of 46.8%.
  • Lesson: Retail panic peaked at the exact market bottom (March 23 at 2,237).
2. Vaccine Monday

Nov 9, 2020 Relief Rally

  • Pfizer announced >90% efficacy.
  • Carnival ($CCL) surged +39.29% in 1 day (dataset record!).
  • Lesson: Sentiment flipped instantly to excitement before revenues recovered.
3. Tesla Retail Wave

Electric Vehicle Euphoria

  • $TSLA gained +743% in 2020.
  • Maintained 64.2% bullishness across 4,341 tweets.
  • Lesson: Retail call buying forced gamma squeezes and upward momentum.
